# Briefing and edition

BLUF: Lead with what needs action. Use bounded sources and a delivery log. Reuse the tested layout.

Installed workflow. Verify the selected mode against its output contract.

## Inputs and modes
Use the requested date, time zone, source allowlist, prior delivery checkpoint, open tasks, and approved section order.
Choose chat brief, lean edition, or full edition. A routine needs separate authorization and the host's routine rules.
Private calendar and inbox context remain in the authorized private workspace. A public template contains no real source content.

## Steps
1. Read the existing production kit and edition log before building. Reuse tested HTML, CSS, fonts, assets, and scripts. Do not rebuild a working layout without reason.
2. Pull new allowed mail since the last edition and the next seven days of calendar. Bound news and job sources to the agreed list. Skip already-delivered stories and empty sections. Do not pad or run broad vault scans.
3. Run a separate 14-day deadline search with terms such as due, expires, RSVP, renewal, and final notice. Filter promos. Incremental newsletter retrieval must not narrow this deadline window.
4. Read each hit in full. Distinguish the due date from the last day to act and from mail arrival. Cross-check open tasks, prior review, calendar, and recent authorized context.
5. Lead with due-today actions, then upcoming items and timed events. Name sources and dates in the private brief. Never call a day clear from the radar alone.
6. Use factual copy with evidence locators. If a value is absent, say Not logged. Check quotations against the selected edition or translation. Do not invent scores, dates, or stories.
7. Apply the audience privacy profile before rendering. Replace restricted event titles with neutral labels when needed. Keep restricted source categories out of any export. Do not include raw identifiers or private source excerpts in a reusable template.
8. For PDF editions, use real images only when permitted. Label generated illustrations. Crop to layout slots without stretching. Apply the agreed image treatment.
9. Lean mode uses one fixed template, text first, and at most one image. Full mode keeps the approved sections and visual features. Do not silently remove requested pages or features.
10. Use product-build-and-release for page-by-page rendering and package checks. Verify reading order, density, contrast, clipping, and dates.

## Output and finish
Deliver the brief or local PDF, evidence notes, source coverage, gaps, and next actions.
Mark stories delivered only after confirmed delivery to the intended user. If delivery fails, preserve pending state.
Do not email, post, print, or share externally without the exact required approval. A prepared edition is not a delivered edition.
