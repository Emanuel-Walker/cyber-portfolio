# Verified visual production

BLUF: Open every source image. Show only what the real source supports. Inspect every delivered asset.

Installed workflow. Verify the selected mode against its output contract.

## Inputs and route
Read the brief, source files, real renders, brand rules, and current channel profile.
Choose placement-only or production mode. Placement-only returns proposals and never edits the target.
For exact screenshot transcription or chronology, use source-to-study-pack.
For specialized story art, also load the relevant visual-director skill.

## Steps
1. Inventory and count every source image. Open each. Record app or subject, visible state, key text, crop limits, and restricted material. Do not infer content from filenames. Indexed count must equal supplied count.
2. Read the target document or product files. Map real pages, screens, dimensions, and features. Mark unsupported features unavailable.
3. Match images by visible concept and state, not their folder of origin. Return section, image, reason, crop or redaction proposal, and unplaced images with reasons. Do not expand restricted details in the report.
4. For production, define the output manifest with names, count, dimensions, format, and transparency. Get one representative style sample reviewed before the batch. Sample approval does not approve the full set.
5. Build from genuine product pages or clearly labeled concept art. Do not invent dashboards, testimonials, results, ratings, or counts. Keep exact wording in editable text layers, separate from generated art.
6. Preserve approved composition and type during targeted fixes. Name each file and the requested change. Avoid collateral revisions.
7. Check every export opens at its requested size. Inspect thumbnail legibility, margins, cropping, transparency, color, and source fidelity.
8. Apply the approved channel profile. The supplied photo profile uses a sheet bounding box of 70 to 90 percent on its larger side, with no side over 90 percent. It excludes navy hues 210 to 240 and checks dHash near-duplicates. Confirm this profile still applies before enforcing it. Similarity is a review flag, not proof of duplication.
9. Run the applicable product-build-and-release checks. Package only inspected outputs. Record missing and unverified assets.

## Output and finish
Deliver the image index, placement proposals or rendered files, source-to-output map, manifest, per-file correction log, and unresolved review items.
Never substitute a mockup for a verified product screen. Do not self-approve or publish.
