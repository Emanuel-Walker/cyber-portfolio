# Workflow 04 - Retroactive Review

**When to run it:** weekly, monthly, quarterly. Also any time you feel disoriented about where your time went.

**ADHD angle:** you do not have to remember what happened. The agent reads what you wrote and surfaces the patterns. No manual tracking required.

---

## Weekly review (15 minutes, every Sunday)

Open the agent and run:

```
Read every daily note in 01-Daily-Notes/YYYY/MM-MonthName/ for the week starting
YYYY-MM-DD. Build a weekly review using templates/weekly-review.md as the shape.

Specifically answer:
1. What did I ship this week?
2. What did I say I would do that I did not do?
3. What is the hyperfixation pattern?
4. What should I say no to next week based on this?
5. One sentence of honest observation about my own behavior.

Save to 01-Daily-Notes/YYYY/MM-MonthName/YYYY-WK-NN_review.md.
```

Review the output. Edit anything that rings false. The agent is reading your words, not your mind, so it will sometimes misread tone. Fix it, and your next review will be sharper.

---

## Monthly review (45 minutes, first weekend of the month)

```
Read every daily note and weekly review from the previous month. Build a monthly review
with the following sections:

- Shipped (projects completed, milestones hit)
- Started (new work that showed up this month)
- Dropped (things I stopped doing, deliberately or not)
- Patterns (hyperfixation themes, repeated no-shows, mood trends)
- Decisions (choices I made that will matter in 3 months)
- One honest paragraph on my own behavior

Save to 06-Archive/Monthly-Reviews/YYYY-MM_review.md.
```

The monthly is where real patterns show up. Decisions you forgot you made. Projects you abandoned without naming the moment. Mood shifts tied to specific weeks.

---

## Quarterly review (2 hours, last weekend of the quarter)

```
Read every monthly review from the past three months and sample 10 random daily notes.
Build a quarterly review with:

- The 3 projects that defined the quarter
- The 1 project I keep saying I will start and have not
- The decision I am most and least proud of
- What I would tell someone else about this quarter if I were giving advice
- Three concrete bets for next quarter

Save to 06-Archive/Quarterly-Reviews/YYYY-QN_review.md.
```

Quarterly reviews are where the vault starts paying real rent. Three months of your own data, read by a tool that does not get bored, is a therapist-grade signal.

---

## Backfill mode (when you missed days or weeks)

Nobody runs this system perfectly. Days get missed. Entire weeks disappear during a hard season. The retroactive backfill workflow is how you come back without starting over.

```
I missed the last 12 days. Help me reconstruct a short daily note for each day.

Sources I will give you:
- My Google Calendar export (attached)
- Git commits from this week (I'll paste them)
- Any text messages I'll share
- Receipts and email summaries

For each day, draft a 4-line daily note with:
- Approximate mood (ask me if you cannot tell)
- One or two things that happened
- A note like "backfilled from calendar + commits on YYYY-MM-DD"

Save each one to the right dated file. Do not fabricate anything you cannot cite.
```

The agent will come back with drafts. You read them, correct anything wrong, keep what rings true, and your time series is intact again. No shame. No streak math. The vault is a tool, not a scoreboard.

---

## The deeper point

Retroactive review is where the compound interest shows up. Writing one daily note is low value. Writing 300 daily notes and having an agent surface patterns across them is where the vault earns its keep.
