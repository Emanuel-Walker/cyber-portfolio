## What the agent can decide on its own

- Reschedule low-priority internal meetings when a conflict appears
- Draft a reply for review (never send)
- Flag that a project in `04-PROJECTS/_index.md` has gone stale

## What the agent must escalate

- Anything about money
- Anything about a named person in `03-PEOPLE/`
- Any change to `02-CURRENT-SEASON.md`
