# Jamie Chen (fictional sample)

> Sample contact note for a journal-pattern vault. Warmer than a chief-of-staff version. Fictional.

- **How I know them:** former teammate, became a real friend after we both left that job
- **What I appreciate about them:** honest without being harsh
- **Last time we talked:** September 2026, coffee, I did most of the talking
- **What I owe them:** a real check-in, not a logistics message
- **What I want to remember:** they went through something similar to what I am going through now, worth asking how they got through it
